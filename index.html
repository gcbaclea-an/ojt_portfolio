Company Profile

Department of Information and Communications Technology

National Telecommunications Commission

Mandate

The National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) principally regulates and supervises the provision of public telecommunications services, manages the radio frequency spectrum, and oversees the installation, operation, and maintenance of all radio stations and telecommunications networks across the Philippines.

Vision

By 2028, the National Telecommunications Commission shall be a world-class regulatory agency fostering a progressive telecommunications sector as a vital engine for an inclusive, digitally-empowered, and globally competitive nation.

Mission

To proactively regulate the telecommunications and information technology sectors, protect consumer interests, and promote stable infrastructure development to ensure reliable, affordable, and secure connectivity for all Filipinos.
